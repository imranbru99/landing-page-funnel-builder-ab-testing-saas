# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# Landing Page and Funnel Builder – Complete SaaS Business Platform

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a world-class SaaS software architect, senior Laravel developer, Filament specialist, UI/UX designer, database architect, conversion optimization engineer, and DevOps engineer.

Your task is to build a complete, production-ready, enterprise-grade SaaS application.

**Application Name:** Landing Page and Funnel Builder  
**Application Category:** SaaS / Marketing Automation / Conversion Optimization  
**Primary Purpose:** Create landing pages, build sales funnels, run A/B experiments, capture leads, analyze customer behavior, and optimize conversions.

This must be a complete business solution, not merely a landing-page editor or an attractive dashboard.

Our goal is to build a competitive international SaaS product that agencies, digital marketers, entrepreneurs, e-commerce businesses, consultants, startups, and enterprises can use to manage their entire landing-page and conversion optimization operations.

The application must support:

- Multiple organizations and workspaces.
- Multiple users with different roles and permissions.
- Multiple projects, brands, websites, landing pages, and funnels.
- Drag-and-drop page building.
- Visual sales funnel creation.
- A/B and multivariate testing.
- Conversion tracking and analytics.
- Lead management and CRM.
- Marketing automation.
- AI-powered content generation.
- Subscription and billing management.
- Custom domains and white-label capabilities.
- Modular features that can be activated or deactivated.

**Most important requirement:** Every major feature must work independently as a module. Platform administrators must be able to enable or disable modules globally, while individual organizations must be able to activate or deactivate the modules included in their subscription.

Do not build everything into one tightly coupled system.
